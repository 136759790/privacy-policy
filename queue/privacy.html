摊位叫号助手 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年6月12日

摊位叫号助手（以下简称“本应用”）是一款专注商户档口排队与高效率叫号的数字化工具。我们深知数据隐私对您的商业经营至关重要，本政策旨在说明我们如何处理您的经营数据。本应用秉承“本地存储、局域网传输”原则，您的所有核心业务数据均存储在您的个人移动设备中，绝不进行任何云端上传。

1. 数据收集与存储

1.1 排值与叫号业务数据：包括您创建的排队流水、营业批次、订单状态（等待、叫号中、已完成、过号）及历史结算明细。这些数据仅用于本地为您进行翻台效率分析及界面展示，全部存储在您设备本地的嵌入式数据库中。

1.2 局域网连接数据：当您开启对外接口让电视（TV）或平板（Pad）连接时，应用会在本地生成微型服务器，利用本地网络广播将数据同步给客户端。在此过程中，本应用不会收集或记录任何连接设备的外部隐私信息。

1.3 数据安全承诺：由于采用完全本地化的存储模式，开发者无法通过任何手段访问或获取您的任何实际店铺经营数据，从源头上杜绝了云端数据泄露和商业秘密外泄的风险。

2. 权限使用说明

2.1 网络与 Wi-Fi 状态权限（ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / INTERNET）：用于检测当前是否连接 Wi-Fi，以便正常开启本地微型服务器。网络权限同时用于可选的应用更新检查、多语言本地化资源包下载或广告内容加载。

2.2 局域网多播广播权限（CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE）：当您希望电视或 Pad 无线联动时，本应用需要使用 mDNS 广播技术以便让局域网内的其他接收端自动发现并绑定这台手机，无需您手动输入复杂的 IP 地址。

2.3 振动权限（VIBRATE）：为了提高高频营业现场的盲操准确度，本应用在叫号、加单、结算等关键节点会申请使用系统振动，以向您提供舒适的触觉反馈确认。

3. 用户数据绝对自主权

3.1 删除与清空：您可以随时在应用内手动删除单条排队记录或执行清空当前波次的操作。请注意，卸载应用将导致所有保存在本地的经营数据随应用一并被系统彻底抹除。

3.2 离线可用：本应用完全不依赖外界互联网运行，即便在无网络或不插电话卡的设备上，依然可以单机使用或组建纯本地局域网（通过无网路由）进行多端叫号。

Privacy Policy for StallQ

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

StallQ (the "App") is a digital utility designed for efficient vendor stall queuing and customer calling. We deeply understand that your business data privacy is critical. The App strictly follows a "Local-Storage First, Local Network Sync" principle. All your core operational and ticket records are stored exclusively on your device and will never be uploaded to any third-party cloud.

1. Data Collection & Storage

1.1 Operational Data: Including ticket numbers, queue sequences, order status (Waiting, Calling, Completed, Overdue), and daily batch settlement histories. This data is stored locally in an embedded database solely to provide UI display and efficiency statistics for your business.

1.2 LAN Sync Data: When you enable the local micro-server feature to link your app with TV screens or iPads, data is transmitted strictly within your local network. No external device privacy, personal information, or network tracking logs are gathered or recorded by the developer.

1.3 Data Security: By implementing fully decentralized local storage, developers have absolute zero access to your retail or dining business records, shielding you permanently from cloud-based leaks or commercial data breaches.

2. Permissions

2.1 Network & Wi-Fi Status (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / INTERNET): Required to detect whether Wi-Fi is active to host the embedded local server. Internet permission is also used for update checks, localization string downloads, or loading ads (if applicable).

2.2 Wi-Fi Multicast Broadcast (CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): Essential when you broadcast your screen to local TV/Pad receivers. The App uses mDNS technology to allow client screens to auto-discover and bind with this server device without tedious manual IP typing.

2.3 Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to trigger tactical haptic feedback on key button taps (adding tickets, calling, settling) to assist smooth and blind operations in fast-paced retail or kitchen environments.

3. Complete Data Sovereignty

3.1 Deletion: You can delete individual records or clear recent business shifts at any time. Please be aware that uninstalling the App will wipe all unbacked-up local databases cleanly from your device.

3.2 100% Offline Usability: The core architecture does not require any active internet connection. You can use it as a standalone counter tool or build a multi-device synced calling station using a completely offline Wi-Fi router.
